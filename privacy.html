Privacy Policy

Ramp Automation Gmail Reader is an internal QA testing tool. It only accesses the Gmail account rampautomationtester@gmail.com, a dedicated test inbox owned by the developer.

The tool reads test emails (such as OTP codes and verification links) during automated tests. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than software testing. Data is not stored beyond the test run.
